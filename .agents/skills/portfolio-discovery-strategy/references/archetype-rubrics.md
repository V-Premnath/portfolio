# Portfolio Archetypes & Strategic Positioning Rubrics

This reference details the 6 primary developer/designer archetypes, their target audiences, recommended visual aesthetics, and content strategies.

---

## The 6 Portfolio Archetypes

### 1. The Creative Technologist / Frontend Artist
- **Target Audience**: Design agencies, Web3/immersive studios, creative directors, avant-garde tech startups.
- **Aesthetic**: Dark mode with neon glowing accents, smooth kinetic typography, custom interactive canvas/WebGL shaders, cursor physics, liquid glassmorphism.
- **Hero Hook Style**: Bold, punchy statement on pushing the boundaries of the web.
- **Key Sections**: Interactive playground/lab, WebGL experiments, visual project showcases with video hover previews.
- **Typography**: Display/Grotesque headers (e.g., *Syne*, *Clash Display*, *Space Grotesk*) paired with clean sans-serif body (*Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*).

### 2. The Systems Architect / Senior Backend Engineer
- **Target Audience**: Engineering VPs, CTOs, enterprise tech recruiters, infra/platform teams.
- **Aesthetic**: Deep dark obsidian theme, high-contrast monospace accents, sleek telemetry graphs, clean minimalist cards, zero visual clutter.
- **Hero Hook Style**: Focus on scale, throughput, reliability, and distributed architecture (e.g., "Architecting systems handling 10M+ daily events").
- **Key Sections**: System architecture breakdown diagrams, performance benchmarks, open-source contributions, technical deep-dive essays.
- **Typography**: Modern monospace headers (*JetBrains Mono*, *Geist Mono*) paired with crisp technical sans (*Inter*, *Roboto*).

### 3. The Full-Stack Product Builder / Startup Founder
- **Target Audience**: Early-stage founders, venture studios, product leaders, client recruiters.
- **Aesthetic**: Modern SaaS aesthetic, sleek border gradients, polished feature cards with badge indicators, live demo previews, glassmorphic UI widgets.
- **Hero Hook Style**: Bridging the gap between engineering and user revenue/growth.
- **Key Sections**: Shipped products with active user counts, revenue metrics, full-stack architecture stacks, live interactive embeds.
- **Typography**: Geometric sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Outfit*, *Manrope*) with high legibility.

### 4. The AI / Machine Learning Engineer
- **Target Audience**: AI research labs, enterprise ML teams, venture capital recruiters.
- **Aesthetic**: Futuristic cyber-mesh gradients, neural node visualizer, sleek mathematical precision, dark slate color palette with electric violet/cyan highlights.
- **Hero Hook Style**: Training, deploying, and optimizing frontier models & intelligent agents.
- **Key Sections**: Model benchmarks, research papers / Hugging Face models, AI demo sandbox, latency/cost reduction metrics.
- **Typography**: Tech-forward sans (*Cabinet Grotesk*, *Space Grotesk*) + *Fira Code*.

### 5. The Minimalist UI/UX Engineer & Craftsperson
- **Target Audience**: Apple/Linear/Vercel-style design-led companies, design systems teams.
- **Aesthetic**: Ultra-clean monochrome with subtle ambient micro-elevation, micro-interactions, flawless typography rhythm, buttery smooth transitions.
- **Hero Hook Style**: Obsession with craft, accessibility, and micro-details.
- **Key Sections**: Design system component showcases, before-and-after interaction videos, case studies on usability improvements.
- **Typography**: Clean Swiss-style typography (*Geist*, *Inter*, *Newsreader* for serif accents).

### 6. The Freelance Consultant & High-End Contractor
- **Target Audience**: SMB owners, marketing directors, agency leads looking to outsource critical delivery.
- **Aesthetic**: Warm executive dark or sophisticated editorial light mode, social proof badges, testimonial carousels, friction-free booking form.
- **Hero Hook Style**: "Helping ambitious brands build high-performing digital products in weeks, not months."
- **Key Sections**: Client testimonials, ROI metrics, process roadmap, Calendly booking integration, FAQ accordion.

## Strategic Discovery Questionnaire Blueprint

When running discovery, present these structured questions to the user:

```text
1. Primary Persona & Role:
   - What is your exact role/title? (e.g., Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Creative Frontend Developer, AI Specialist)
   - Which archetype above best aligns with your vision?

2. Primary Target Audience:
   - Who is the main person you want to impress? (e.g., Big Tech hiring managers, seed-stage startup founders, freelance clients)

3. Flagship Projects (Top 2-4):
   - What are your best 2 to 4 projects to feature?
   - What were the tech stacks, key challenges, and measurable results for each?

4. Primary Call to Action (Conversion Goal):
   - What should a visitor do before leaving? (e.g., Book a meeting on Calendly, Send an email, View GitHub/LinkedIn, Download Resume)

5. Visual Tone & Mood:
   - Preferred mood: (A) Cyberpunk / High-Tech Neon, (B) Minimalist Linear/Vercel Dark Mode, (C) Sleek Creative Glassmorphic, (D) Editorial Swiss Clean?
```
